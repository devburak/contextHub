# Compare ContextHub

ContextHub is designed around one question: how can an agency or multi-brand team run
many tenant-scoped content operations without cloning the CMS for every customer,
brand or site?

These guides compare architecture and operating model, not just feature checklists:

* [ContextHub vs WordPress Multisite](./wordpress-multisite.md)
* [ContextHub vs Strapi](./strapi.md)
* [ContextHub vs Payload](./payload.md)

Every product here can be a good choice. The useful distinction is whether your team
needs a website network, a developer framework, or a tenant-aware content platform
with shared operational capabilities.

Comparisons were last reviewed on 2026-09-21. Product capabilities change; follow the
linked official documentation before making a final platform decision.
