# Privacy notice

Effective date: 22 August 2026

For ContextHub Cloud account and billing data, the service provider and data controller
is **İKONX Bilişim ve Tarım Sanayi ve Ticaret Ltd. Şti.** Registered identity and contact
details are published in [Legal, service, and merchant identity](./legal-merchant-identity.md).

## Data and purposes

Depending on the relationship, ContextHub may process account identity and contact data,
tenant memberships and roles, billing name and address, country, tax identifiers,
support communications, agreement records, payment-provider references, security logs,
device/IP data, and service-usage records.

This data is processed to establish and perform the service contract, manage tenant
access, collect payment, invoice and keep accounts, provide support, secure the service,
prevent abuse and fraud, meet legal duties, and establish or defend legal claims.
Billing forms rely on contract formation/performance, legal duties, and legitimate
interests as applicable; this notice is not a request for consent.

## Recipients and international transfers

Necessary data may be shared for these limited purposes with payment institutions,
authorized resellers/Merchants of Record, invoicing and accounting services,
infrastructure and security providers, professional advisers, and authorized public
bodies.

To provide hosting, backup, security, support, CDN, and edge delivery, personal data may
be stored, cached, processed, or transferred outside Türkiye, including in EU/EEA
countries. Appropriate transfer mechanisms under Article 9 of Türkiye's Personal Data
Protection Law and applicable technical and organizational safeguards are used.
Distributed edge locations process only the connection, device/IP, security-log, and
cache data necessary to serve and protect requests.

## Payment data and retention

ContextHub does not store full card numbers or card security codes. Those values are
entered on and processed by the authorized payment party's secure interface. Provider
event payloads are minimized, protected, and retained only for the documented billing,
audit, dispute, security, and legal periods.

Retention depends on the record type and applicable legal duties. When a subscription
ends, tenant data is not automatically deleted at the same moment; export, retention,
and deletion follow the applicable service and account-closure workflow.

## Your requests

Send access, correction, deletion, objection, and other applicable data-subject requests
to `support@ctxhub.net`. Sufficient information may be requested to verify the requester
and locate the relevant record. Corporate contact details are also available at
[ikon-x.com.tr/en/contact](https://ikon-x.com.tr/en/contact).
