# About us

ContextHub is a headless content and data management product for teams that need to
manage content, media, collections, forms, and digital experiences inside a tenant
boundary and deliver them to multiple channels through APIs.

ContextHub Cloud is operated at `ctxhub.net` by **İKONX Bilişim ve Tarım Sanayi ve
Ticaret Ltd. Şti.** The company's corporate website is `ikon-x.com.tr`; the product and
subscription domain is `ctxhub.net`. Both domains identify the same legal service
provider.

## What we provide

- Tenant-scoped content, media, collection, form, and role management
- An API-first, headless delivery model
- Webhook, cache, and distributed edge/CDN flows
- Semantic Search and managed commercial capabilities on paid plans
- A documented boundary between the Community edition and managed ContextHub Cloud

A tenant represents one site or a separate content context. Where appropriate, separate
languages or operational areas of the same site may use separate tenants. Subscription,
user, and quota rights apply to the selected tenant; there is no separate “number of
sites per plan” counter.

## Community and ContextHub Cloud

The ContextHub Community core is available for open-source development and self-hosted
use. ContextHub Cloud adds hosting, operations, security, backup, edge delivery,
support, and the managed or commercial capabilities included in the selected plan. See
[Pricing and plans](./pricing-and-plans.md) for the current comparison.

## Infrastructure and data regions

For availability, performance, security, backup, and disaster recovery, the service may
use distributed cloud, security, CDN, and edge infrastructure in Türkiye and abroad,
including EU/EEA countries. The processing and international-transfer framework is
described in the [Privacy policy and notice](./privacy-notice.md).
