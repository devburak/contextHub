## Collection and invoicing roles

Depending on billing country and transaction type, collection may be performed by an
authorized payment institution or an authorized reseller/Merchant of Record for digital
products. Before payment, the secure checkout identifies the collecting party, currency,
taxes, renewal terms, and any buyer terms that apply.

Where an authorized reseller/Merchant of Record is the legal seller for a transaction,
it may issue the corresponding invoice in its own name. Where a payment institution only
processes the payment, its payment record or receipt is not the statutory invoice; the
invoice is issued by İKONX or its authorized e-invoice service under the applicable rules.

The appearance of a payment institution, card scheme, or authorized reseller on a bank
statement, receipt, or invoice does not change the fact that ContextHub Cloud is operated
by İKONX Bilişim ve Tarım Sanayi ve Ticaret Ltd. Şti. or transfer product-support
responsibility away from it.

ContextHub does not store card numbers or card security codes. Card information is
processed on the authorized payment party's secure interface.

Send access, correction, and other data-subject requests to

## Document and domain verification policy

Service terms, privacy, cancellation/refund, features, and pricing information are made
available to customers on `ctxhub.net`. The corporate site `ikon-x.com.tr` should clearly
identify ContextHub Cloud as a product operated by the company and link to this page.

Payment is initiated only from an approved ContextHub product domain or the secure domain
of an authorized payment party. If you see an unfamiliar domain or unexplained statement
descriptor, verify it with `support@ctxhub.net` before completing payment.
